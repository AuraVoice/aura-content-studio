# Store and platform rules

Researched 2026-11-21 from the official pages linked below. Re-check a page before relying on a
rule for a new campaign; update this file (with the date) when a rule changes.

## Google Play feature graphic (1024 x 500)

Sources: [Add preview assets](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151),
[Graphic assets](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/1078870)

- JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha, exactly 1024 x 500, up to 15 MB.
- Avoid: device imagery, store badges, the words "Best", "#1", "Top", "New", "Free", and pure white, black or dark gray backgrounds (they blend into Play).
- Keep the logo, name, slogan and key UI centred, away from the edges; Google shows cutoff zones on a sample image without pixel sizes, so keep about 60 px clear on every side.
- With a promo video attached the graphic becomes its cover and a play button sits in the centre. Keep the exact centre free of text.

## Google Play promo video

Same sources as above.

- A YouTube URL (public or unlisted), monetization off, not age restricted.
- Show the real app within the first 10 seconds; at least 80% of the video should be real app use.
- Only the first 30 seconds autoplay, muted: captions must carry the story with the sound off.
- No close-ups of fingers tapping the screen unless core use happens off device.
- No "Download" or "Try now" wording.
- Landscape or portrait both work; a portrait video must not have black bars.

## Reels, Shorts, TikTok (1080 x 1920)

- Platform UI covers the top and bottom of the frame. Keep text and key UI between y = 270 and y = 1536 (top 14%, bottom 20% reserved), and away from the right edge where the action buttons sit. `studio.json` holds these numbers and `hyperframes check` flags violations per platform.

## Category lessons (AI companion apps)

From the 2026-11-21 competitor review (Replika, Nomi, Pi, Character.AI, Gemini, ChatGPT Voice, the Friend pendant):

- What converts: being heard and being remembered without re-explaining. Replika and Nomi both lead with memory; Pi leads with "time for you".
- What people doubt: whether memory is real, privacy, the company changing or shutting down their companion. Showing the editable "Your Aura" memory screen answers the first two directly.
- Avoid: romance imagery, AI-generated human faces, an AI that narrates someone's life uninvited (the Friend pendant backlash), sad lonely-person framing, "#1/Best/Free", and a voice demo with no task in it.
